How is accuracy calculated?

Each missing, replaced, or extra word counts as one difference. Accuracy = 100 × (1 − differences / transcript words), with a minimum of 0%. Words are counted after normalization; spelling mistakes still count as replacements.

Case, spaces, and ordinary punctuation are ignored. Unambiguous contractions such as don't = do not and whole numbers from 0 to 20 are supported. An apostrophe inside a word matters: we'll and well are different. Ambiguous 's and 'd forms are not expanded.

This is transcription accuracy, not a rating of your English level or meaning comprehension. Python calculates differences; AI gives separate English feedback and does not assess pronunciation from typed text.
